Good to know

Which computers does Nori run on?

Macs running macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer — Apple Silicon and Intel — and Windows 10 or 11 PCs. One purchase includes both.

My computer showed a security warning when I opened it.

Mac: double-click Sushiesta, close the warning, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Windows: on the "Windows protected your PC" screen, click More info → Run anyway.
You only need to do this once.

Is this a subscription?

Nope. Pay once, keep Nori forever, with free updates.

I bought it on my phone — how do I get it on my computer?

Your receipt email has download links for both Mac and Windows. Open that email on your computer and download the version you need.

Lost your download link?

Your receipt email from Polar links to your downloads page — open it anytime to download again, on any computer.

Does Nori collect any data?

No. Your settings and stretch history never leave your computer. Nori only checks whether your mic or camera is in use, so he doesn't interrupt meetings — he never listens or watches.
