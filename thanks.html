Sushiesta

Nori's on his way! 🎉

Check your email from Nori (via Polar) and click Access purchase — that's where you'll find downloads for both Mac and Windows. Bought on your phone? Open that email on your computer.

On a Mac

  1. Download Sushiesta-for-Mac.dmg, open it and drag Nori into Applications.
  2. Open Sushiesta from Applications.
  3. Nori moves into your menu bar and says hi.

On Windows

  1. Download and run Sushiesta-for-Windows.exe.
  2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Find Nori by the clock — if he's hiding, click the ^ arrow and drag him onto the taskbar.

Can't find the email? Search your inbox (and spam) for "Polar" or "Sushiesta".
